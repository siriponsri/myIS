# Abstract

This manuscript specifies a controlled adaptation-surface study for
family-level patent retrieval. It compares frozen instruction and seed-skill
controls with matched-budget SkillOpt, SkillOpt-Lite, and typed HarnessOpt arms
after a frozen candidate-recovery harness. The protocol isolates editable skill
and harness surfaces, preserves a protected joint-test boundary, and reports
paired retrieval, cost, latency, and failure outcomes without legal claims.
