This manuscript evaluates whether mechanism-grounded CrossRoute candidate
recovery improves family-level OUT-domain patent retrieval under a fixed
candidate budget. The study separates a zero-tuned six-route recipe from a
bounded metric-tuned variant while holding query views, prompts, encoder, and
reranker instructions fixed. The zero-tuned arm combines TAC,
independent-claim, and grounded-mechanism views through paired lexical and dense
routes. The metric-tuned arm may change only route enablement, quotas, fusion,
reciprocal-rank fusion, and pool or rerank depths within the same raw budget.
The protocol uses shared seed-42 query membership with independent Track C and
Track S evaluators, manifests, artifacts, budgets, and data firewalls. Its
preregistered primary comparison is C1 minus C0 on OUT Recall@100; comparisons
with the hybrid control are additional Holm-family analyses. A frozen-pool
ranking analysis is diagnostic only and cannot establish a separate ranking
claim. Results and claims remain unavailable until protected evaluation,
aggregate validation, and the applicable Owner decision are complete. The
system is decision support, not legal advice.
