# Goal 001: A9 publication and release

Assemble a journal-ready manuscript, reproducibility package, figures, tables,
and public release from validated aggregate-safe A0-A8 evidence after
`D3_SUBMIT_RELEASE`. The paper must distinguish A5 confirmatory evidence, A6
operational/full-corpus evidence, A7 diagnostics, and A8 post-confirmatory
reranker evidence. Raw corpus, qrels, membership, identifiers, rankings,
per-query outcomes, credentials, provider payloads, and model payloads are
never included.
