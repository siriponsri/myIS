# Productization Boundary

ArmIndex separates the research plane from the product plane so a strong
non-commercial research arm is never presented as a deployable default.

## Research plane

- benchmark experimentation under frozen contracts;
- PatEmbed-large only inside its research/non-commercial boundary;
- protected evaluator and one-shot Selection;
- immutable manifests, receipts, cost ledgers, and negative findings;
- research champion selected from all eligible research arms.

## Product plane

- commercial-capable BM25, BGE-M3, Snowflake, and Qwen profiles;
- deterministic tenant/private indexes and runtime policies;
- latency, cost, reliability, security, and observability limits;
- explicit deployment and data-retention boundary;
- model, dependency, index, and redistribution license review;
- commercial-capable champion selected independently.

## Readiness

- Research architecture: ready for A0 fixture verification
- Implementation migration: in progress
- Measured ArmIndex campaign: pending
- Production validation: pending
- Commercial release: not authorized

This repository does not claim that ArmIndex is ready to sell or deploy.
