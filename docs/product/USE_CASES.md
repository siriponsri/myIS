# Use Cases

## Patent prior-art retrieval

Retrieve family-level candidate evidence while preserving protected qrels and
separating retrieval evidence from legal conclusions.

## Legal research RAG

Map frozen logical representation fields to legal documents, report
unsupported mappings, and validate transfer independently from patent results.

## Contract and policy retrieval

Compile sections, clauses, definitions, and provenance into deterministic
retrieval units suitable for private indexes.

## SOP and regulatory search

Use stable field order, section identity, and source spans to support auditable
retrieval over long structured procedures.

## Enterprise structured documents

Select a latency and cost profile appropriate to a tenant boundary while
retaining immutable model, representation, harness, and index lineage.

All use cases remain targets until measured and operational validation exists.
