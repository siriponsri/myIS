# A1.2 Publication-Impact Preregistration V13

V13 is an additive hardening revision. It preserves V12 byte-for-byte and
binds its file and self SHA-256. It also preserves the U011 DAPFAM literature
digest byte-for-byte while reconciling metric scope: the source study reported
NDCG@100 primary, whereas ArmIndex A1.2 is a candidate-exposure and
representation-screening study whose primary development outcome is OUT
Recall@100. OUT nDCG@100 and OUT nDCG@10 remain secondary ranking outcomes.

This is a scope-specific metric supersession, not a rewrite or reclassification
of historical evidence. It does not adopt V11, authorize a provider, open a
GPU workload, access protected data, measure retrieval, expose Selection or
Final, or make a publication claim.

V13 freezes every nested analysis field rather than relying on descriptive
booleans. It requires pre-registered representation-program contrasts,
retriever-family grouped-arm contrasts, and retriever-by-representation
transfer interactions. Claims are limited to the five frozen arms, five
programs, DAPFAM, and the frozen budget.

The future A1.2 screen remains development/diagnostic evidence. REP-DEV and
HARNESS-DEV remain exploratory; Selection-125 is one-time finalist selection,
not confirmation; Final-872 is the sole confirmatory evaluation. A publication
claim requires the future frozen Final receipt.

Candidate exposure uses OUT Recall@100 under frozen family aggregation and
requires equal total candidate depth for single-arm and union comparisons.
Oracle metrics are frozen-pool diagnostics only. Complementarity requires
eligible-query denominators, unique relevant family-query-pair numerators,
pairwise overlap definition, equal-depth union comparison, and incremental
latency/cost. Arm promotion cannot use a maximum individual cell.

Operational results require frozen cold/warm, repetition/order/discard,
hardware/runtime, resource-sampling, and failure-denominator procedures.
The protected final analysis must use exactly 10,000 paired bootstrap resamples
and report aggregate-safe effect and confidence-interval outputs.
