# Draft cover letter - World Patent Information

Dear Editor,

Please consider the manuscript "Retriever-Conditioned Representation Search
for Cross-Domain Patent-Family Retrieval" for publication as a research
article in *World Patent Information*.

The study examines representation choices within frozen retrieval systems. We
introduce a bounded search over executable patent-text programs while holding
each retriever fixed during its search. The evaluation separates development,
a single selection exposure, a paired 872-query confirmation, and later
diagnosis. The selected PatEmbed RCRS system achieved OUT Recall@100 of
0.4425, compared with 0.3311 for the prespecified frozen FAST system that
combines BM25 and Arctic (paired difference 0.1114; 95% bootstrap confidence
interval 0.1023-0.1204). This is a comparison of two complete frozen systems,
not an isolated effect of representation. A later diagnosis of the unchanged
winner shows that most strict OUT-relevant family pairs are absent from its
Top-200 pool, separating candidate exposure from ordering within the pool.

The manuscript connects patent-family information retrieval, auditable
evaluation practice, and operational limits relevant to patent-search
systems. Its claims are limited to the tested protocol. The work makes no
claim to a new embedding model, universal state-of-the-art performance, or
direct comparability with external protocols. Negative and tied development
results, licensing constraints, and a worse p99 latency outcome are reported
alongside the Final-872 result.
