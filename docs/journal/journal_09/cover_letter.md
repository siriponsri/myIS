Dear Editor of *World Patent Information*,

Please consider our manuscript, "Beyond the retriever: candidate exposure, not
ranking, limits AI-based cross-domain patent search," for publication in
*World Patent Information*.

The article fits the journal's scope through its focus on patent search practice
and the evaluation of AI-based retrieval systems. Rather than treating a model
name as the full unit of comparison, the study evaluates complete retrieval
configurations and identifies where their remaining recall error occurs.

The article reports three main findings. First, coarse deterministic
construction choices do not stably reorder the five retrievers studied. Second,
a configuration frozen before held-out evaluation raises Recall@100 from 0.331
to 0.442, a paired gain of 0.111. Third, candidate exposure is the dominant
measured constraint in the confirmed configuration: 78% of relevant families
do not enter its Top-200 pool, perfect reordering can add at most 0.072
Recall@100, and extending the retained ranking to depth 1,000 adds 0.341. These
results motivate configuration-level reporting for AI-assisted patent search.

An earlier and shorter version of this work was submitted to iSAI-NLP 2026.
The present article extends that submission with pool-depth analysis, a
technology-section breakdown, failure cases, and practice-facing implications.
This manuscript is original and is not under consideration elsewhere.
