# Draft cover letter — World Patent Information

Dear Editor,

Please consider the manuscript “Retriever-Conditioned Representation Search
for Cross-Domain Patent-Family Retrieval” for publication as a research
article in *World Patent Information*.

The study tests whether patent-document representation can be fixed
independently of the retriever. We introduce a
bounded search over executable patent-text programs while holding retrieval
weights fixed. The evaluation separates development, a single selection
exposure, a paired 872-query confirmation, and post-confirmatory diagnosis.
The selected system improves OUT Recall@100 from 0.3311 to 0.4425 in the
confirmatory comparison (paired difference 0.1114; 95% bootstrap confidence
interval 0.1023–0.1204). A subsequent diagnosis of the unchanged winner shows
that most strict OUT-relevant family pairs are absent from its Top-200 pool,
distinguishing candidate exposure from within-pool ranking.

The manuscript connects patent-family information retrieval, reproducible
evaluation practice, and operational limits relevant to patent-search
systems. Its claims are bounded to the tested protocol. The work makes no
claim to a new embedding model, universal state-of-the-art performance, or
direct comparability with external protocols.
Negative and tied development results, licensing constraints, and a worse
p99 latency outcome are reported alongside the confirmatory gain.
