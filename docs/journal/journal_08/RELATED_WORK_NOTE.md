## Why
A reviewer for this journal will look for the journal's own recent retrieval
literature. Its absence invites the question "why are recent World Patent
Information papers missing?" before any scientific objection is raised. Four
references were added, with a short paragraph in Section 2.2 that ties them to
what this paper measures.

## Two entries need a human check before submission
`references/references.bib` carries a `note = {TODO(human): ...}` field on the
Rees and Wirz entry and on the survey entry. Open each article page, copy the
volume, article number and full author list, then delete the note field. The
note prints in the reference list, so it cannot be missed.

The survey entry is the one to check most carefully. The ScienceDirect listing
pairs titles and author lines in a way that could not be resolved from search
results alone, so the author names may belong to a neighbouring article.

Body text moved from about 5,114 to about 5,252 words. The journal invites
papers of roughly 3,000 to 6,000 words, so there is still room, but any further
addition should replace text rather than extend it.
