# iSAI-NLP 2026 Submission Guidelines

## 01 Submission

### 1. Paper Format

* All authors must use the official **IEEE Manuscript Templates for Conference Proceedings**, which can be obtained from the IEEE Manuscript Templates for Conference Proceedings.
* Submissions must not exceed **6 pages**, including:

  * Figures
  * Tables
  * Appendices
  * Other supplementary content included in the manuscript
* All submissions must be submitted in **PDF format**.

### 2. Formatting Requirements

Submissions must strictly adhere to the IEEE conference proceedings formatting instructions specified above.

Alterations to spacing, font size, margins, or other formatting elements that deviate from the official instructions may result in **desk rejection without further review**.

### 3. Authorship Policy

When uploading the submission files, authors must ensure that the submission system correctly recognizes the **complete list of authors**.

The addition of authors to the submission system after the review process is not permitted.

By submitting to iSAI-NLP 2026, all authors acknowledge that they comply with the IEEE authorship policy.

### 4. Plagiarism and Concurrent Submission Policy

By submitting to iSAI-NLP 2026, authors acknowledge that they are aware of and agree to comply with the **IEEE Plagiarism FAQ**.

Papers submitted to iSAI-NLP 2026:

* Must not have been published elsewhere.
* Must not be under review elsewhere.
* Must not be submitted for review to another venue while under consideration by iSAI-NLP 2026.

Any violation of the concurrent submission policy will be considered a serious breach of scientific ethics, and appropriate action may be taken.

The conference chairs reserve the right to:

1. Share the list of submissions with the Program Committee Chairs of other conferences whose review periods overlap with iSAI-NLP 2026.
2. Use external plagiarism-detection software, under contract with IEEE, to identify possible violations of these policies.

### 5. Double-Anonymous Review Process

iSAI-NLP 2026 will employ a **double-anonymous review process**.

Submissions must not reveal the identities of the authors. Authors must make every reasonable effort to preserve anonymity throughout the review process, including the following:

* Omit all authors’ names and affiliations from the submitted manuscript.

* Write citations to the authors’ own related work in the third person.

  Example:

  * Acceptable: “The prior work of XYZ demonstrated that...”
  * Not acceptable: “Our prior work demonstrated that...”

* Do not include URLs that link to author-identifying websites, tools, source-code repositories, datasets, or project pages.

* Anonymize company or organization names that could reveal the authors’ identities.

* Provide only the general organizational characteristics necessary to understand the context of the study.

* Do not state that the manuscript has been submitted to iSAI-NLP 2026 when uploading a preprint to arXiv or a similar platform.

* Use a different title for any preprint uploaded to arXiv or a similar platform during the review process.

* Ensure that acknowledgments do not reveal the origin of the work or the identities of the authors.

### 6. Compliance and Author Responsibility

Any submission that does not comply with these requirements may be **desk rejected without further review**.

It is the sole responsibility of the authors to ensure that the following requirements are satisfied at the time of submission:

* IEEE formatting requirements
* Six-page limit
* PDF submission requirement
* Double-anonymous review requirements
* Authorship policy
* Plagiarism policy
* Concurrent submission policy
* Any other requirements specified by iSAI-NLP 2026

## 02 Accepted Contributions

Upon notification of acceptance, all authors of accepted papers will be required to complete an electronic **IEEE Copyright Form**.

Accepted authors will also receive further instructions for preparing the camera-ready version of their papers.

The following information in the camera-ready version cannot be changed from the submitted version without explicit approval from the iSAI-NLP 2026 Chair:

* Paper title
* Authors’ names
* Authorship list

All accepted contributions will be published in:

* The conference’s electronic proceedings
* IEEE Xplore

> **Important:** At least one author of each accepted paper must register for and present the paper at iSAI-NLP 2026 for the paper to be included in the conference proceedings.

IEEE has implemented policies that allow papers whose authors do not present at the conference to be excluded from further distribution, including exclusion from IEEE Xplore.

iSAI-NLP 2026 applies this policy and reserves the right to prevent papers that are not presented at the conference from being published in IEEE Xplore.
