# iSAI-NLP Paper Rules and Template Contract

## Scientific rules
- Use the evidence pack as the numeric authority.
- Keep A5 confirmation separate from A6/A7 post-confirmatory diagnosis.
- Label A7 oracle values as frozen-pool analytical bounds, never reranker results.
- Keep raw incidence counts separate from macro-Recall quantities.
- Do not claim external protocol comparability, causal attribution, reranker efficacy, pool-expansion efficacy, or external generalization without evidence.

## Review rules
- English, A4, official IEEE conference two-column, at most 6 pages.
- Use `IEEEtran` conference formatting; do not edit margins or font sizes to fit content.
- Keep author, affiliation, funding, repository, institution identifiers, and acknowledgements that reveal identity out of the review version.
- Every figure/table must be cited and trace to evidence.
- Keep references real and verifiable; unresolved metadata must be checked before submission.
