# G1 Reproduction Evidence Checklist (Draft Only)

This is a preparation checklist, not an approval record, decision, or authorization.
G1 remains `pending` until the Owner creates an immutable decision through the
typed decision process.

- [ ] Corpus commitment is supplied and reviewed.
- [ ] Query commitment is supplied and reviewed.
- [ ] Qrels commitment is supplied and reviewed outside the agent workspace.
- [ ] Family commitment is supplied and reviewed.
- [ ] Evaluator commitment is supplied and reviewed.
- [ ] Field protocol is supplied and reviewed.
- [ ] Published reproduction targets are supplied and reviewed.
- [ ] Compute budget, provider, endpoint, data egress, and fallback policy are supplied and reviewed.
- [ ] Exact split membership/hash is supplied through the protected Owner process and reviewed.
- [ ] Exact OUT-positive counts are computed by the protected Owner process and reviewed; estimates are not accepted.
- [ ] Reproduction authorization scope is explicit and binds F1.1 only.
- [ ] Frozen canonical RunSpec and all required hashes exist after authorization.

No item may be checked by inference from a Dashboard, MLflow, Brain, or prior Gate.
