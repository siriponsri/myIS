# Figure QA

Inspect the rendered figure at actual paper size.

PASS only if:
- text is readable;
- no collisions/clipping;
- panel labels are unambiguous;
- legends are short and visible;
- axes/units are complete;
- colors remain interpretable in grayscale;
- caption agrees with plotted values;
- visual emphasis matches scientific importance;
- no panel is redundant;
- figure still makes sense when skimmed before reading the body text.

Also ask:
Could this be a smaller table or two sentences?
If yes, the figure may not earn its area.
