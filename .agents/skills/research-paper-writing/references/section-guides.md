# Section guides

## Abstract
Problem -> gap -> method -> evaluation -> key supported result -> bounded implication.
Avoid background that does not change interpretation.
Avoid citations unless necessary.

## Introduction
1. concrete problem
2. why it matters in this setting
3. what prior approaches leave unresolved
4. exact research question/boundary
5. what this study does
6. specific contributions

Do not begin with a generic "AI has transformed..." paragraph.

## Related Work
Synthesize by tension, assumption, capability, or unresolved boundary.
Do not write an author-by-author catalogue.

## Method / Experimental Design
Explain only the machinery needed to understand and reproduce the study.
Use prose/pseudocode for procedural logic when that is clearer than equations.

## Results
Lead with the answer to the RQ, then the evidence.
Do not narrate every row of a table.
Separate observation from interpretation.

## Discussion / Limitations
State what the evidence supports, what it does not support, and the most plausible boundary.
Avoid generic limitation boilerplate.

## Conclusion
No new evidence.
State the learned result, bounded contribution, and one useful next step.
