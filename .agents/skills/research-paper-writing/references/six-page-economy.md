# Six-page information economy

Never shrink the IEEE template to rescue overflow.

Protect space in this order:
1. research question and central contribution
2. validity/evaluation design
3. method detail needed for interpretation/reproducibility
4. central result
5. limitation needed to calibrate the claim
6. related work/background
7. secondary diagnostics

Cut first:
- generic background;
- duplicated motivation;
- repeated numerical prose;
- literature catalogues;
- decorative figures;
- table columns that do not affect a comparison;
- equations that merely formalize obvious operations.

A page is successful when its purpose is obvious and its highest-value evidence is visually discoverable.
