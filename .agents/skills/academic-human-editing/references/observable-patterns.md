# Observable patterns to flag — never authorship claims

Flag only when the pattern harms clarity or credibility:
- formulaic academic openers;
- generic significance inflation;
- repeated contrast templates;
- automatic three-part lists;
- repeated transition starters;
- em-dash pileups;
- paragraph-length uniformity that makes argument structure mechanical;
- vague attributions ("research shows") without source;
- synonym restatement with no new evidence;
- promotional adjectives;
- conclusions that sound broader than the experiment.

Do not use banned-word lists mechanically.
A technical term is not bad because models often use it.
Context and proposition quality matter more than detector folklore.
