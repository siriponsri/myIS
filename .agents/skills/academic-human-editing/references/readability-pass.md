# Readability pass

For each sentence ask:
- What is the main verb?
- Who/what does it apply to?
- What condition qualifies it?
- What evidence supports it?
- Can one clause be removed without losing the claim?

Prefer:
- concrete subjects;
- verbs over nominalizations;
- one main proposition per sentence when the logic is dense;
- short explicit links between evidence and interpretation;
- familiar field terminology.

Do not force active voice everywhere.
Passive voice is appropriate when the procedure/object matters more than the actor.

Do not force sentence-length variation for its own sake.
Rhythm should follow argument complexity.
